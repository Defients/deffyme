# Defdef’s Gatehouse — future artwork brief

**October 10, 2026 · America/New_York · Prompt only; no new artwork generated**

Authority: [Gatehouse specification](../docs/GATEHOUSE_SPEC.md). This supersedes `room-prompt.md` for future homepage art. The old room prompt remains provenance for assets already in the preview. Concept #3 was selected from the referenced feedback, but its image is unavailable here; this brief expresses the agreed relationship rather than reconstructing that image.

## Environmental artwork prompt

Create a wide 16:9 illustrated environmental backdrop for deffy.me: a distinctive gatehouse or passport desk that belongs to Defdef, the canonical character who hosts and operates access to Deffy’s creative domain. Premium painterly architectural depth, charcoal/ink surfaces, warm brass and amber authority cues, restrained violet distance, cream-lit readable negative space. One purposeful front desk/console and a clear threshold; a coherent domain visible beyond it. Reserve a prominent character station at the desk, with the architecture and light guiding attention toward that station. Leave quiet areas for real HTML identity, a short host line, actions and six readable destination links. Convey an authored personal site with wit and spatial confidence. Avoid a generic fantasy game lobby, dense dashboard, cozy creator-bedroom premise and a wall of competing portals.

Reserve one small localized root-layer seam beneath or behind the desk for a contained red Creature anomaly. Keep all other decorative lighting amber/gold/violet. The seam should suggest a controlled surface interrupted from below, rather than an exploding universe or wall-to-wall corruption. Red effect occupies no more than roughly 5% of the normal desktop composition. No flashing, illegible text, baked-in navigation, logos, readable signs, fake status counters or watermark. The environment is fictional illustration, never a photograph of Deffy’s actual room.

## Character and UI composition

Compose supplied canonical Defdef separately at the reserved station, large enough to read as gatekeeper/host. Preserve crown, silhouette, fur/color cues and supplied appearance. Do not ask a background generator to redraw him from description. If creating a composite mockup later, inspect and provide the canonical image reference first. The resting derivative can support an attentive desk layout; richer supplied poses are optional.

UI labels are actual HTML: Meet Deffy, Projects / The Forge, Astrizda, Play, Listen, Now. Suggested actions are “Request Access — Choose a destination” and “Just Looking Around.” The anomaly label is “THE CREATURE OF THE INTERNET.” Do not bake those labels into the backdrop or imply that environmental art alone supplies working links.

## Responsive derivatives and delivery

Desktop reference: 1920×1080, with about one-third quiet space for text/directory and a prominent Defdef station. Also review 1440×900. Mobile needs a purposeful compact station crop, leaving identity and links in ordinary flow; do not crop a wide painting until Defdef disappears or shrink desktop text into art. The mobile seam sits after the directory and cannot obscure controls.

Supply environment and character as separable layers with reserved dimensions, responsive derivatives and transparent padding where appropriate. Preserve source masters outside public output. Record generator/source, date, approval, transformations, dimensions, encoded bytes and SHA-256 in the existing provenance pipeline. Test hero plus active Defdef against ≤300 KiB mobile / ≤500 KiB desktop transfer before accepting art. No asset acceptance or performance result is implied by this prompt.
