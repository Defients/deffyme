I’m Deffy Pyah Urz, from New Hampshire. A lifelong PC gamer, aspiring streamer, self-taught creator, and someone who keeps asking whether the defaults really have to stay that way.

World of Warcraft and League of Legends have been part of my life for years. So have computers, internet friendships, and the question “what if?” Sometimes that question becomes a game. Sometimes it becomes software, music, or an entire fictional universe.

I like making things, but I don’t need every experiment to become useful, profitable, or particularly sensible. Sometimes an idea is just too ridiculous to leave alone.

The person behind it.

I care about individuality, autonomy, meaningful relationships, and intellectual honesty. I’m curious about psychology, philosophy, artificial intelligence, and what people could become. I also like taking a ridiculous premise much too far.

That doesn’t make me consistent. I’ve changed my mind, contradicted myself, and failed to live up to things I value. There’s room for that here, too.
