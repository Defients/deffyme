# Unaided recognition worksheet — not yet run

Use five unfamiliar viewers. Obtain their own responses; do not substitute agent inference. Show final V2.4 idle at the viewer's representative viewport, initially hiding role labels, caption, footer and narrative. Avoid coaching and avoid saying “operator” or “control” before their answer.

1. Ask: “What do you think this creature is doing, and what in the image makes you think that?” Record their exact words and where they point. Score idle recognition only if they independently describe operation/authority and locate the input/conductor relationship.
2. Show inspection and the provisional pointing acknowledgment. Ask: “What changed, what did you learn, and where would you go next?” Score broken-versus-received signal comprehension and a sensible Worlds destination without hints.
3. Ask about distractions and the receiver’s foreground occlusion. Record qualitative feedback separately; do not redefine success after seeing results.

Threshold: at least 4/5 idle recognition, and at least 4/5 transmission/destination comprehension, as specified in the V2.3 handoff. Record device/viewport and any readability or image failure. This is a small formative study, not statistical proof.
