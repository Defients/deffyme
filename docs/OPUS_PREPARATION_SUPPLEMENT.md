## Three consequential weaknesses; corrections in value order

1. **Operation remains asserted more than shown.** Align the station junction, existing paw and local gold response as one readable causal chain. Review it with labels disregarded at desktop and mobile sizes. No character redraw or executable behavior is needed for this decision.
2. **Digital specificity and its mobile continuity are weak.** Give the existing conductor/fracture a small, bounded treatment of segmented channels or broken scanline fragments; retain physical depth and contained red. Frame the same anomaly and junction together on mobile. Use acknowledgment gold more selectively so its change is distinguishable from permanent trim.
3. **Mobile hierarchy delays meaning and personal connection.** Keep immediate header navigation; test a compact destination explanation before optional discovery. On Human, place the first approved Story paragraph before secondary links, reduce repeated excerpts, and retain the approved identity-distinction paragraph. Preserve the quiet surface and reading measure. Do not invent personal copy.

Corrections are proposals, not implementation or measured estimates. The initial preparation pass needed no supplemental artwork or prototype. The bounded continuation below tests one correction with static geometry only.
