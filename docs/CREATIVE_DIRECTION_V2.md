## Purpose and emotional journey

**Spectacle earns attention. Discovery earns curiosity. Humanity earns connection.**

Deffy.me is an authored digital place through which visitors discover Deffy's work, humor, contradictions and fictional worlds. The opening has one powerful focal point; the next encounter rewards attention; the human introduction lowers the intensity enough for an actual conversation to begin. Projects become evidence of a creative mind rather than the entrance's dominant directory.

The intended visitor can be a returning project user, an unfamiliar visitor, a curious explorer or someone interested in the person behind the work. Each gets ordinary navigation immediately. Discovery supplies optional depth, never a test of patience or competence.

Keep intensity asymmetric: arrival is visually striking but concise; Defdef's territory is playful and controlled; the human area is intimate and readable; the Forge is materially expressive but efficient; Astrizda invites further depth; archives expand only with intentionally published material. Quiet is part of the art direction.

## Recommended experience architecture — awaiting approval

Use **a compact entrance with a short optional domain encounter and calmer dedicated destinations**, rather than committing to six full-screen homepage chapters. The conceptual sequence describes pacing, not a mandatory scroll order. All destinations are directly accessible.

| Zone | Proposed placement | Content priority / navigation |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival — The Signal | Existing `/` entrance, evolved after approval | One composed threshold, clear Deffy identity, purposeful Defdef presence, immediate Human / Projects / Worlds links, utilities and motion controls. |
| The Creature's Domain | A distinct, bounded homepage encounter after the arrival; no new route in the first slice | Defdef-owned machinery, contained red anomaly, one optional reaction and an ordinary Human link. Its native disclosure works as a static fallback. |
| Meet the Human | `/story/`, linked prominently from arrival and domain | Existing first-person introduction; achievements, fallibility and humor only from approved material. No archive narrative imported automatically. |
| The Forge | `/forge/`, direct from header and arrival | Existing three-work collection and URL-backed filters; later full catalog only through reconciled records. Status and launch destination remain explicit. |
| Astrizda and wider worlds | `/astrizda/` | Clear fictional-world gateway; concise explanation and existing destinations; no lore knowledge required. |
| Archives / deeper discovery | Deferred architecture, not a new output route | Creative history and personal history remain distinct; exact approved entries precede any archive index, attachments or search exposure. |
| Listen, Now, Connect, Site Privacy | Existing `/music/`, `/now/`, `/connect/`, `/site-privacy/` | Utility/secondary destinations; retain null-media and link-only states without pretending unfinished features exist. |

**Express:** Arrival → Human / Forge / Astrizda / utilities, in one ordinary link activation. Direct links bypass the arrival. **Discovery:** Arrival → optional Defdef encounter → Human → chosen artifact or world → approved depth later. Every page offers an ordinary return-home link and useful navigation; completion or visitation state never controls eligibility.

Retain the six Gatehouse destinations as an available useful directory, but prioritize three readable choices (Human, Projects, Worlds) in the composition. Play, Listen and Now can be secondary destinations. This is a recommendation for later hierarchy changes, not permission to alter `gatehouseDestinations` now. Preserve `/#meet` compatibility when future layout changes occur.

No new `/creature/`, `/explore/` or archive route is required for the first proof. A dedicated creature route is deferred until the bounded encounter demonstrates enough substantive content to justify ownership and maintenance.

## Defdef: operator, not explanatory assistant

Show agency through **cause and effect**. A visitor deliberately activates a small piece of Defdef's machinery; the local signal reorganizes; his supplied pose or a gold signature acknowledges the change; the environment settles. He appears to operate it because the reaction is spatially tied to his station and uses a consistent visual signature. This is staged fiction and deterministic UI behavior, not autonomous intelligence or new character lore.

Keep his first presence immediate but restrained: silhouette/pose, crown-light and an occupied threshold communicate ownership. The fuller territory encounter comes moments later or by explicit selection. He need not remain visible on deeper reading pages. Remove the expectation of a constantly explaining front-desk assistant; use at most one short authored line in an encounter.

**Recommended first behavior:** one native disclosure exposes a small signal artifact and ordinary text. Opening it changes only a contained decorative pattern; optionally select a supplied curious/pleased pose after derivative review. Close returns to idle. No game engine, pointer surveillance, LLM, polling, timer-driven interruptions, score, inventory, unlocks or visitor identity collection. New host copy is proposed UI writing, never quoted character canon.

Canonical imagery controls anatomy and crown detail. Preserve the actual gold crown and its existing jewel/eye colors, black shadow silhouette, magenta/violet edges, chest tuft, tail and claws. Environmental red is a separate layer: do not recolor the creature or remove legitimate warm/red details already present in the master. No automatic mirroring of asymmetric art. Supplied still poses do not establish a walking rig or animation frames.

## Visual system

Retain the usable ink/cream/violet/amber foundation and licensed Inter/Space Grotesk typography. Increase authorial composition through scale, space and lighting rather than more UI chrome. Proposed zone accents: violet darkness at arrival; purple/magenta, crown gold and one red interruption in Defdef's domain; quieter neutral surfaces and warm light for the human; restrained material/index cues in the Forge; optional world imagery in Astrizda. These are art directions, not new token values approved for code.

- One dominant visual focal point per encounter. Layer foreground, inhabited middle distance and quiet background; HTML headings and actions sit on stable opaque/near-opaque surfaces.
- Desktop art review at 1920×1080 and 1440×900: confident heading, clear character station and substantial negative space. Never require a fixed viewport height or clip enlarged content to fit a fold.
- Use readable body copy around the existing 17px baseline and a 60–75-character reading measure. Important choices and descriptions should not inherit the current 9–11px decorative metadata treatment. Small labels are subordinate and dispensable.
- Red anomaly: one clipped region, default static; preserve the prior proposed ≤5% initial-viewport decorative-red target as a review constraint. Fine broken scanline/column fragments may evoke a corrupted signal without a full Matrix rain field. No real-text scrambling, global flicker, false errors or broken hit areas.
- Gold marks ownership and acknowledgment sparingly. Avoid arbitrary neon gradients, dashboard counters, dense particles, decorative controls that imply functionality, or scenery containing baked-in UI labels.
- Warm imagined-room art stays preserved. Recommend potential reuse as a quiet deeper-page vignette after review, not the arrival premise or a documentary image of Deffy's home. Its retention does not oblige reuse.

## Transitions and interaction contract

Recommend a **settling signal**: the localized anomaly stops, saturated light narrows to a violet/gold mark, and the next readable surface becomes visually quieter. Use composition and repeated marks to establish continuity across ordinary page loads. No SPA router or page-transition framework is required. The Human route is available immediately even if that transition is never observed.

| Pattern | Enhanced behavior / bounded cost | Keyboard, touch, reduced-motion and failure behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Domain disclosure | One explicit reaction; ≤300 ms transform/opacity sequence in Full; no loop | Native `details/summary`, Enter/Space, touch activation, visible focus. Essential/OS reduce: static open state. No JS: complete text and links. Escape restores summary focus through existing dismissal behavior. |
| Operator acknowledgment | At most one active response; optional reviewed still-pose swap, one adjacent prefetch maximum | Same activation/focus meaning across devices; no hover-only content, no live announcement of decorative pose changes. Image failure leaves textual identity and navigation. |
| Arrival-to-human continuity | Shared small visual mark; optional opacity/color feedback on the initiating control | Actual anchor navigates immediately. Modifier-click, new tab, Back, direct entry and no JS work normally. No animation waiting gate. |
| Artifact exploration | Labeled real project link and optional native detail | Project title/status/launch available in initial HTML. Existing Forge filter remains URL/back-forward aware; full list works without hydration. |
| Optional secret | Nonessential authored decorative variation, deferred beyond first proof | No required content, personal history or navigation hidden inside it. Provide visible discovery equivalent if it carries any meaningful information. |

Essential/Balanced/Full keep equal content. System reduced motion overrides stored preference on load and change; failed storage does not block use. Balanced gets brief control feedback only; Full can add one measured response. Cancel pending optional work on hidden-tab, offscreen and preference changes; ignore overlapping activation, return deterministically to idle and clean up listeners/timers. Do not add a general state framework for these few states.

No autoplay sound. Sound design is deferred: if later justified, explicit enablement, mute, selected-media provenance and a complete silent equivalent are prerequisites. No audio download before opt-in.

## Mobile and accessibility

Mobile carries the same **identity and causal story**, not the same panoramic composition. At 390×844 and 320px narrow, show a compact crown/host station, readable identity and one useful action early; large links follow in normal flow; the domain's anomaly is a short strip/disclosure after choices. At 768 portrait, simplify into a station plus list/grid. Do not require all six choices above a phone fold or use shrunken scene hotspots.

Preserve landmarks, skip link, logical headings/DOM order, screen-reader usability and visible focus. Minimum design target 44×44px for interactive targets; verify 200% text and narrow reflow. Text contrast targets: 4.5:1 normal, 3:1 large; required non-text boundaries 3:1 on actual composited backgrounds. Decorative layers are hidden from assistive technology and do not intercept input; meaningful character art has concise alt text. Color, sound, drag, hover and motion never convey essential meaning alone.

Static mode is deliberately composed, not a blank experience with effects switched off. Limited hardware uses the static scene; no capability inference is needed to deliver its content. Any later richer effect must fit the same budgets and have a complete static fallback.

## Engineering contracts and costs

Keep Astro, existing TypeScript/Tailwind build and selective Forge React island. No dependency or renderer migration is recommended. Native HTML plus scoped CSS and a small optional controller can deliver the first proof.

Retain existing ceilings: homepage HTML ≤35 KiB gzip; homepage JS ≤45 KiB gzip; shared CSS ≤25 KiB gzip; fonts ≤100 KiB total / two files; Forge JS ≤150 KiB gzip; active environment plus Defdef art ≤300 KiB mobile / ≤500 KiB desktop; each active pose ≤140 KiB compressed and ≤4 MiB decoded; total decorative decoded image memory ≤40 MiB. Keep LCP ≤2.5 s and CLS ≤0.1 under recorded cold-load profiles; ≤200 ms interaction target is a lab target, not field INP certification. Attribute decorative main-thread tasks over 50 ms. Existing Gatehouse long-task concerns remain unresolved.

Worth the cost: one carefully composed responsive environment, canonical optimized character, native artifact interaction, bounded opacity/transform feedback, strong typography and static spatial depth. Defer or reject continuous glitch rain, GPU-heavy blur stacks, parallax layers that demand per-frame layout, full 3D, persistent particles, pointer-following characters, route-transition machinery and soundscape playback. Added complexity must make Defdef's agency or the visitor's understanding demonstrably clearer.

Keep `src/content/routes.json` unchanged. The source already proposes biography `/story/` while legacy records declare a `/story` → `/projects/story/` alias; target-host slash normalization and redirect precedence must be tested before publishing. Preserve `/projects/story/` and reviewed `/storytime`; no redirect is emitted or host configuration changed here. Product `/privacy/` stays separate from `/site-privacy/`; only `music/index.html` is owned within existing music paths. No whole-tree upload or delete-sync.

## Ten design decisions and tradeoffs

| Question | Recommended position | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| 1. First Defdef encounter? | Immediate visual presence; richer territory moments later and optional. | Strong identity without making visitors endure a mascot introduction. |
| 2. Operator without engine? | One local input → signal response → pose/signature → idle sequence. | Less mechanical scope, stronger authored causality; no emergent game simulation. |
| 3. Warm room? | Preserve as historical art and possible quiet vignette, not the entrance. | Avoids wasted work while acknowledging that cozy-room staging undersells V2. |
| 4. Spectacle to human? | Settle the signal and carry one shared mark into a quieter ordinary route. | Coherent pacing without continuous cinematic navigation; not seamless 3D movement. |
| 5. Forge artifacts? | Material framing and concise creation context around explicit project/status/link rows. | Character without sacrificing scanning, direct launch or filtering. |
| 6. Above fold? | Whose site, Defdef ownership cue, Human/Projects/Worlds choices and utilities. | Useful content wins over filling the view with spectacle; extra discoveries follow naturally. |
| 7. Smallest prototype? | Review existing Gatehouse compositions, then one isolated domain artifact and Human exit. | Validates ownership and pacing, not the whole site's final artwork. |
| 8. Worthwhile effects? | Static depth, one bounded localized response, responsive canonical art. | Invest in composition; continuous spectacle and complex rendering deferred. |
| 9. Mobile parity? | Equivalent character/agency and direct links in a compact vertical composition. | Same creative identity with fewer visual layers and no desktop hotspot dependence. |
| 10. Conflicting documents? | Supersede Gatehouse persona-only rupture, constant host/tour-guide framing and strict no-home-encounter assumptions; preserve actual G1 record and engineering rules. | Latest brief governs intent without pretending earlier implementation never happened. |
